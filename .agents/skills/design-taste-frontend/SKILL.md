# tasteskill v2 (design-taste-frontend)

A page built by a model usually looks like the average of every page the model has seen: a centred headline over three lines, a little label above it, a row of icon cards, a soft gradient, a few thin rules and a monospace caption. Nothing in it is wrong and nothing in it was decided.

This skill replaces those defaults with decisions:

1. **The idea comes from the subject**: In the study, what separated the best sites from the forgettable ones was a concept taken from the thing itself, not the styling.
2. **A house style with no ornaments**: Enforce strict anti-slop house rules.
3. **Substance over thinness**: Taking ornaments away is only half the job; a page that has lost its labels, rules and cards and gained nothing in their place reads as thin. The other half is substance: type set larger, whole fields of colour, real pictures of this subject's world, and every section carrying its full narrative content.
4. **One live thing**: Give the page one thing the visitor can do, or that is different for each visit, made from the idea. It uses the subject's real content, works on a phone with a thumb, has a plain fallback, and never blocks reading.

## Page vs App Screen
- **A page** is read and scrolled: marketing site, landing page, portfolio, product showcase.
- **An app screen** is worked in: dashboards, admin views, lists with filters, forms. Follows quiet borders, high scannability, real cohesive data, and clean purposeful buttons.

## Anti-Slop House Rules
- NO generic purple/indigo AI gradients or pastel bubbles.
- NO 3-card generic feature grids without narrative.
- NO lorem ipsum or fake invented statistics/press logos.
- Strict typographic hierarchy: expressive display font for titles/artisanal flavor, crisp high-legibility sans for UI.
- Genuine subject motifs: authentic materials, textures, origins, and craft rituals.
